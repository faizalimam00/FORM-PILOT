# Code of conduct

FormPilot welcomes people who want to build useful, understandable tools. Treat contributors and users with respect. Critique work and ideas without attacking people. Be patient with questions, explain assumptions and make room for different levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, publishing someone else’s private information and persistent disruptive promotion are not acceptable. Do not post profile exports or screenshots containing personal data.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause a discussion, or restrict participation to protect the community. Decisions should be proportional, explained where practical and protect the privacy of affected people.

Before public launch, the maintainer must publish a private conduct-reporting contact alongside the security reporting route. Until then, request a private route without describing sensitive details publicly. This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests and project community spaces.
